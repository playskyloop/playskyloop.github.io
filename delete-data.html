Delete my data

You can delete everything Skyloop Drones has stored about you, at any time.

Fastest: in the game

  1. Open the menu and choose Settings, then scroll to Privacy. Or use the save screen.
  2. Tap Delete my data, then Yes, delete. (No is selected first, so you can't delete by accident.)

This deletes, straight away:

  • your Challenge scoreboard entries, current and past
  • any Challenge prize money waiting for your saves
  • your bug reports, including any files and voice notes
  • every career save and setting on that device
Career saves can't be brought back. If you want to keep one, use Export on the save screen first.

By email

If you no longer have the game, or want us to do it for you, email us. Include your player name, and your player ID if you have it (it is shown in the Delete my data box in the game). We'll delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

This opens your email app with the request filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there.

Or write to playskyloop@gmail.com directly.

What isn't kept anyway

Multiplayer positions are only held during a match, and internet addresses are not saved. Server logs that mention a player name are removed automatically after a few weeks. See the privacy policy.

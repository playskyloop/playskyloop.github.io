Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Skyloop Drones is a small game made by one family. This page explains, in plain English, what the game keeps and why. We keep as little as we can.

Things we never do
  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics tools in the game
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing data for marketing
  • No chat. Players can't send each other messages.
  • No loot boxes and nothing random to buy. The Google Play version has one optional purchase, the Full game (see below). The web version has no purchases at all.
  • No accounts or passwords. No location, contacts or photos are read unless you choose to attach a file to a bug report.

What stays on your device

Your career saves (money, drones, jobs, settings) are stored only on your phone or in your browser. We can't see them. Export and Import make a save file that you keep yourself.

What our server stores

The game has one small online server. It is used for multiplayer, the weekly Challenge scoreboard and bug reports.

  • Player name: the name you pick. It is shown to other players in multiplayer and on the Challenge scoreboard. Rude names are replaced with "Guest". Please don't use your real full name.
  • Player ID: a random code made by the game on your device, for example p3f9c…. It links your scores to your device without knowing who you are.
  • Challenge scoreboard: your best lap time, number of laps and drone speed for each Challenge. If you play the Challenge from a career save, the save's random Challenge ID is kept too, so prize money can reach that save.
  • Multiplayer: while you play online, the server knows your name, your drone and where it is flying. That is held in memory for the match only. The server log notes the name when someone joins, so we can fix problems.
  • Internet address (IP): used for a few minutes to stop spam (for example, too many bug reports at once). It is not saved.

Bug reports

The Report a bug form is optional. If you send one, it includes:

  • what you type, and your email address only if you choose to give it, so we can reply
  • any files you attach (such as screenshots), and a voice note only if you record one. The microphone is only used while you press record in that form.
  • your player name, your player ID, and basic device details: the Android or browser version, the screen size and which screen of the game you were on

Bug reports are delivered to our own Google account (Google Sheets, Drive and Gmail) so we can read them. Google stores them for us under its own privacy terms.

The Full game purchase (Google Play version only)

The Google Play version is free up to the intermediate drone parts. A one-off Full game purchase opens the rest (advanced parts, hosting multiplayer games and the Space Force missions). Joining friends' games is always free, and nothing you have already earned is ever taken away.

  • Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card, bank or Google account details. Google's own privacy policy covers the payment.
  • When you buy, the game may send the purchase's Google Play purchase token (a long random code, not your name or card) to our server, which asks Google whether it is a real, paid purchase. The token is checked and then forgotten. It is not saved or logged.
  • The game remembers on your device that you own the Full game. Restore purchases asks Google Play again, for example on a new phone. If a purchase is refunded, the Full game locks again, but your saves, parts and progress stay.

How your data travels

When you play online, the app talks to our server over an encrypted connection (https and secure websockets). The server runs on our own computer at home, reached through Tailscale.

Games on the same Wi‑Fi (Nearby games, where one phone hosts) connect directly between the devices on that network and don't go through our server. That local connection is not encrypted, so other devices on the same Wi‑Fi could see the game traffic: player names, drone positions and race results. Only play Nearby games on a Wi‑Fi network you trust. Nothing from a Nearby game is sent to us.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings → Privacy → Delete my data (also on the save screen). It asks you to confirm first. Then it deletes your Challenge scores, waiting prize money and bug reports from our server, and clears the saves on that device. You can also email us. See Delete my data.

Children

Skyloop is made to be fine for all ages. It has no chat, no ads and no loot boxes, and the only thing other players see is your chosen player name. The Google Play version has one optional Full game purchase; Google Play can ask a parent to approve purchases (Family Link or purchase authentication in the Play Store settings). Parents can ask us to delete a child's data at any time using the email below.

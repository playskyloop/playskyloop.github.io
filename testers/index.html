Become a tester

Help us test Skyloop on Android before it launches. Testers get the full game free while testing is on.

Step 1: Join the testers group

Join with your Google account. Use the same one that's signed in to the Play Store on your Android phone.

Join the testers group

Step 2: Install from Google Play

Open the testing link on your phone, tap Become a tester, then install Skyloop from the Play Store.

Open the testing link

Please keep it installed for at least 14 days and have a fly now and then. It really helps us get Skyloop launched.
Just joined the group? It can take a few minutes before the testing link works. If it doesn't, wait a bit and try again.

Good to know

  • Android only for now.
  • Questions or feedback: playskyloop@gmail.com, or use Report a bug in the game menu.
  • Privacy: joining the group shares your Google account email with the Skyloop team only, so we can give you access. See our privacy policy.
